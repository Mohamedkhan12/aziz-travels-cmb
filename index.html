Cancellation

Clear, fair policies

  • 1–6 days before arrival: 100% charged
  • 7–13 days: 50% charged
  • 14–28 days: 25% charged
  • Beyond 28 days: decided by the hotel case by case

Refunds & guarantees

Refund requests made within 72 hours of purchase, before the order is processed, are considered under our cancellation policy and processed within 21 days. Once the itinerary is approved, any hotel changed without your written consent is fully refunded. Guests should hold a valid passport and visa, and tell us in advance about medical or wheelchair needs.
